About LitReview

LitReview is an open repository of literature reviews generated with artificial intelligence, serving the basic sciences. Think of it as arXiv for AI-written syntheses: every review has an identifier, an abstract page, and is available as LaTeX source, PDF, and HTML.

Why

Keeping up with primary literature is increasingly impossible for individual researchers. Carefully prompted language models can scan large corpora and draft structured syntheses that a domain expert then curates. LitReview publishes those syntheses openly so that anyone — students, researchers, practitioners — can get a rapid, honest overview of a field's state.

How reviews are made

  1. A topic within a scientific area is selected.
  2. An AI model surveys the literature and drafts the review.
  3. The draft undergoes human curation before publication.
  4. Reviews are versioned: identifiers follow the arXiv convention YYMM.NNNNN.

AI-generated content can contain errors or fabricated citations. Always verify citations against the primary literature.

Areas

  • Ecology & Evolution — active
  • Physics — coming soon
  • Further basic sciences are planned.

Open by design

All reviews are free to read and download. The site itself is open source at github.com/alrobles/litreview.org.
